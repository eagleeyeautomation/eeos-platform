# EEOS flagship sales script

EEOS exists because leaders must make connected decisions from fragmented operational signals. It is not another CRM: it consumes authorized evidence, preserves its source and confidence, connects it through the Brain, and presents bounded recommendations for a human decision.

The live story uses only the synthetic Summit Home Services Group. Business Health identifies South Carolina staffing risk, Virginia as a benchmark, and Florida conversion opportunity. The Brain separates facts, derived metrics, inferences, assumptions, and recommendations. Business Memory and the Knowledge Graph preserve authorized context and relationships without crossing tenant boundaries. Executive Copilot answers from evidence and refuses unsupported questions.

Executive Automation prepares governed work. A recently MFA-authenticated administrator must approve it, the decision is audited, and external email, SMS, CRM, financial, and connector execution remains blocked. C2C, C2B, and B2B cards are review-only opportunities. Industry Packs configure terminology and measures on one platform; previews do not change saved configuration.

Live today: the certified synthetic scenario, guided modules, evidence-backed recommendations, governed approval, Memory, Timeline, audit attribution, reset/replay, and external-execution blocking. Configurable: Industry Packs and authorized organization context. Future roadmap must be described only as roadmap, never as a live capability.

Never claim guaranteed growth or revenue, perfect predictions, autonomous business control, unsupported integrations, or an outcome not shown by evidence.
