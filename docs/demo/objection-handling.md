# Objection handling

**Is this another CRM?** No. EEOS is the governed intelligence and decision layer. It can use authorized operational evidence while leaving systems of record in their proper role.

**Does the AI run the company?** No. The Brain explains evidence and recommends. Protected actions require human approval, and this certified demo blocks external execution.

**Can it guarantee growth?** No. Recommendations are confidence-bounded and outcomes must be measured.

**Does information cross customers?** No. Tenant and location isolation remain enforced. Platform governance shows privacy-safe aggregates rather than tenant-confidential operations.

**Are all integrations live?** Claim only the connectors and states visible in the authorized environment. A preview or roadmap item is not a live integration.

**Is the demo real?** The platform workflows are live; the organization, locations, metrics, and opportunities are deliberately synthetic.
